WELCOME TO THE COATWOOD

Make yourself small.
Dream a little bigger.

Your village lives on a sleeping giant. Gather scraps, build homes and workshops, and let your villagers collect supplies while you’re away.

A whole world in 3D. Drag to rotate the giant all the way around, including his back. Switch Rotate / Move to pan instead. Use Village view, Giant’s face, or Meet crew to get closer.

Gather gently. Every 2 pokes gives 1 thread, 1 twig, and 1 seed. Pinch or scroll to zoom. Tap the giant to poke him. Every 3 pokes adds 1% wake-up; every 6 pokes earns a clothing coin. Use See giant for a full-body view. Tap buildings for upgrades. Workshops make noise; hush the village to calm things down.

When the meter fills, he rolls onto another side and your village starts over. Coins and clothes stay with you. Fill it again and he sits up, opens his eyes, rubs them, and goes back to sleep. That second wake-up keeps your village.
